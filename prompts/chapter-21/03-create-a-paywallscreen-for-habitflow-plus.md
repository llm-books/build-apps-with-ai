# Create a PaywallScreen for HabitFlow Plus

**Chapter 21 — Making Money From Your App**  
Used in: Step 3

---

```
Create a PaywallScreen for HabitFlow Plus. It should clearly list what Plus unlocks (unlimited habits, full statistics, custom themes, data export), show the yearly and monthly prices fetched from RevenueCat with the yearly plan highlighted as the best value, offer a 7-day free trial, and include a "Restore Purchases" button for people who already paid on another device. Match HabitFlow's calm purple design and keep the tone encouraging, never pushy or guilt-inducing.
```
