# Bump HabitFlow's version to 1

**Chapter 22 — Maintaining And Updating HabitFlow**  
Used in: Step 3

---

```
Bump HabitFlow's version to 1.0.1 (patch release). Update the version field in app.json, increment the Android versionCode by 1, and increment the iOS buildNumber by 1. These numbers must increase with every store submission or the stores will reject the upload.
```
